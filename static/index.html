How it works

Three steps. No legal knowledge needed.

1

Open any Terms page

Sign-up pages, privacy policies, app terms. The AgreeWise badge appears in the corner.

2

Click the badge

AI reads every clause and tags it safe, caution or risky.

3

See the risk

A Low / Medium / High score, risky lines highlighted on the page, and a summary in English or Tamil.

Install in one minute

Install it straight from this site, no Chrome Web Store account needed:

1

Download

Download the zip and unzip it.

2

Open extensions

Go to chrome://extensions in Chrome.

3

Developer mode

Turn on Developer mode (top right).

4

Load unpacked

Click Load unpacked and pick the agreewise-extension folder. Done.

Want to test it? Open our sample Terms page after installing.
